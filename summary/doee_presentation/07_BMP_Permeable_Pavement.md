# 07 – BMP Specifications: 3.5 Permeable Pavement Systems

**Source:** `docs/doee presentation/07_BMP Specifications_3.5 Permeable Pavement.pptx`

## Overview
Permeable pavement allows stormwater to pass through the surface and infiltrate or be stored in a subsurface reservoir layer. Used for parking lots, plazas, low-traffic roads, and sidewalks.

## Surface Material Options
- Permeable pavers (interlocking)
- Pervious concrete
- Plastic grid pavers
- Porous rubber
- Porous asphalt

## Design Versions

| Version | Description |
|---|---|
| **Standard** | No underdrain; relies on full infiltration |
| **Enhanced without Underdrain** | Deeper reservoir for full infiltration credit |
| **Enhanced with Underdrain** | Underdrain with infiltration sump; partial infiltration, partial storage |

## Feasibility Criteria
- External CDA:permeable pavement ratio ≤ **5:1** (2:1 recommended)
- CDA to the pavement should be impervious
- Additional criteria: water table depth, minimum head, setbacks, slopes

## Conveyance & Pretreatment
- Large storms must be managed via overdrains, overflow inlets, or extra storage depth / underground detention
- No pretreatment required if CDA is 100% impervious

## Design Specifications

| Layer | Specification |
|---|---|
| Reservoir Layer | No. 57 or No. 2 stone; sized for design storm |
| Underdrains | PVC with 3/8-inch perforations; must drain practice in 48 hours |
| Infiltration Sump | No. 57 or No. 2 stone; must drain in 48 hours |
| Filter Layer | No. 8 choker stone for optional separation |
| Observation well | Required |

- Bottom slope must be as flat as possible (< 2%); internal flow barriers may be used on sloped sites

## Drainage Time Equations
```
t_d = d_p × η_r / K_sat    [must be < 2.0 days]

Sv = A_p × t_f × K_sat     [storage volume]
```
- Assumes t_f = 2 hours (0.083 day) to fill reservoir layer

## Retention Value Calculations

| Version | Retention Value |
|---|---|
| Standard | 5.0 CF per 100 SF of practice area + accepted TSS removal |
| Enhanced without Underdrain | 100% of storage volume in reservoir layer |
| Enhanced with Underdrain | 100% of storage in infiltration sump layer + additional 5.0 CF per 100 SF |
