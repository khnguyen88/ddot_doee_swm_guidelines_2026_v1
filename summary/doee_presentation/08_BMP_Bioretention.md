# 08 – BMP Specifications: 3.6 Bioretention

**Source:** `docs/doee presentation/08_BMP Specifications_3.6 Bioretention.pptx`

## Overview
Bioretention is the most widely used BMP in DC. It captures and filters runoff through an engineered planting media. Multiple design configurations and sizes are available for different site conditions.

## Bioretention Types

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **B-1** | Traditional Bioretention (standalone cell) |
| **B-2** | Streetscape Bioretention (linear, curbside) |
| **B-3** | Engineered Tree Pits |
| **B-4** | Foundation Planters |
| **B-5** | Residential Rain Gardens |

## Feasibility Criteria
- Works for all soil types and most site conditions
- Requires 4–5 feet of hydraulic head
- No irrigation or baseflow from the cell
- Liner required for hotspot areas (contaminated soils, etc.)

## Conveyance & Pretreatment
- Can be configured **off-line** (bypass for large storms) or **on-line** (overflow device required)
- Required pretreatment options: pretreatment cell, grass filter strip, stone diaphragm

## Design Options & Retention Values

| Design Option | Requirements | Retention Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | ≥ 18" media; underdrain | 60% of design storm captured |
| **Enhanced (with Underdrain)** | ≥ 24" media; 9" minimum infiltration sump; sump drains in 72 hr (K_sat = 0.1 ft/day) | 100% of design storm captured |
| **Enhanced (full Infiltration)** | ≥ 18" media; full storage volume infiltrates in 72 hr | 100% of design storm captured |

All designs also provide accepted TSS removal. Oversizing a standard design may allow it to meet full retention requirements.

## Design Criteria

### Dimensions
- Ponding depth: **3 inches minimum, 18 inches maximum**
- Side slopes: 3:1 or vertical drop
- Observation well: required

### Filter Media Specifications (by weight)
- 80–90% sand
- 10–20% silt and clay (max 10% clay)
- 3–5% organic amendments
- Phosphorus concentration: 5–15 mg/kg (Mehlich I) or 18–40 mg/kg (Mehlich III)

### Maximum Media Depth
Governed by runoff coefficient of the CDA (Rv_CDA) and the SA:CDA ratio. See Table 3.20 in the Guidebook.

### Sizing Equation
```
Sv = (SA_bottom × d_media × η_media) + (SA_bottom × d_gravel × η_gravel) + (SA_average × d_ponding)
```
- η_media = 0.25 (effective porosity, filter media)
- η_gravel = 0.40 (effective porosity, gravel/underdrain layer)

## Engineered Tree Pit (B-3) Specific Requirements
- Filter media must be connected to media running under the sidewalk
- Average ponding depth ≥ 3 inches
- Sand-Based Structural Soil (SBSS) is permitted as filter media with same phosphorus limits; if compacted beyond DDOT max, assign porosity of 0.10

## Surface Cover Options
- Mulch with perennial vegetation (most common)
- Turf
- Stone with perennial vegetation

## Landscaping Criteria
- A planting plan must be submitted
- Consider maintenance requirements, plant suitability, and native species
- Landscaping is critical to bioretention hydraulic function
