# 01 – Stormwater Management Requirements

**Source:** `docs/doee presentation/01_Stormwater Management Requirements.pptx`

## Overview
General compliance training covering when DC stormwater rules apply, how to calculate SWRv, and the full compliance workflow from plan submission to approval.

## When Rules Apply

| Trigger | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Major Land-Disturbing (MLD)** | ≥ 5,000 SF land disturbance AND (any pre-project natural cover OR ≥ 2,500 SF post-project impervious/BMP) |
| **Major Substantial Improvement (MSI)** | Construction costs ≥ 50% of pre-project assessed value AND combined structure + disturbance footprint ≥ 5,000 SF with same land cover conditions |

## Stormwater Retention Volume (SWRv)

```
SWRv (gal) = P/12 × (0.95 × I + 0.25 × C + 0.0 × N) × 7.48
```
- **P** = Precipitation (in) — determined by regulatory rain event
- **I** = impervious cover area (SF); Rv = 0.95
- **C** = compacted cover area (SF); Rv = 0.25
- **N** = natural cover area (SF); Rv = 0.0

## Regulatory Rain Events
- **MLD activity:** 1.2-inch storm
- **MSI activity:** 0.8-inch storm
- Sites with both triggers calculate SWRv separately for each land cover type using the applicable rainfall depth.

## Quantity Control (Detention) Requirements
- **2-year storm (3.14 in):** Control peak discharge to pre-development conditions
- **15-year storm (5.23 in):** Control peak discharge to pre-project conditions
- **100-year storm (8.34 in):** Control peak discharge to pre-project conditions — only if development increases SFHA or may cause flooding to a building

## Exemptions
| Category | Exempt From |
|---|---|
| Athletic playing fields, permeable tracks/playgrounds | Retention |
| Major Substantial Improvement; PROW | Detention |
| Disturbance for BMP installation; utility work | Retention & Detention |

## Practicability Processes Available
- Affordable Housing
- Trails
- Small structures at parks

## Key Compliance Concepts
- Understand difference between SDA (Site Drainage Area) and CDA (Contributing Drainage Area)
- Know BMP basics from Guidebook Chapter 3
- Understand PROW MEP process
- Know what Offv (offset volume) is and how to comply
- Access Stormwater Guidebook System (SGS) at doee.dc.gov/swguidebook
