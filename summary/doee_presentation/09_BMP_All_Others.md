## Overview
Quick-reference overview of the remaining BMP types in Guidebook Chapter 3 beyond Green Roofs, Rainwater Harvesting, Disconnection, Permeable Pavement, and Bioretention.

## 3.14 Tree Planting & Preservation — Retention Values

### Preserved Trees
| Tree Size | Retention Value |
|---|---|
| Small (avg. mature spread < 40 ft) | 10 ft³ |
| Large (avg. mature spread ≥ 40 ft) | 20 ft³ |
| Special (circumference > 44 in.) | 30 ft³ |
| Heritage (circumference > 100 in.) | 40 ft³ |

### Planted Trees
| Tree Size | Retention Value |
|---|---|
| Small (avg. mature spread < 40 ft) | 5 ft³ |
| Large (avg. mature spread ≥ 40 ft) | 10 ft³ |

### Rootable Soil Volume Requirements
- **Small trees:** 600 ft³ single planting; 400 ft³ each in shared space
- **Large trees:** 1,500 ft³ single planting; 1,000 ft³ each in shared space
- Soil considered rootable to 3 ft deep and 16 ft (small) or 27 ft (large) radius
- Rootable soil must be connected (not blocked by compacted subbase)

## Proprietary Practices (3.13) — Approval Process
- Submit field studies and/or lab test data
- Approval follows NJDEP Protocol
- Generally yield low retention value with high TSS removal
- Details in Appendix T of the Guidebook
