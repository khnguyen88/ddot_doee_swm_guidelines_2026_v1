# 04 – BMP Specifications: 3.2 Green Roofs

**Source:** `docs/doee presentation/04_BMP Specifications_3.2 Green Roofs.pptx`

## Overview
Green roofs are a preferred BMP in high-intensity redevelopment areas and are a major element of compliance at dense urban sites. Two types: extensive (shallow media, low maintenance) and intensive (deeper media, heavier loads, more vegetation options).

## Feasibility Criteria
- Sufficient structural roof capacity
- Roof pitch must be appropriate
- Required setbacks from HVAC equipment and building edges
- Compliance with building and fire codes
- Contributing drainage area to the green roof ≤ 100% of green roof area
- Flow dissipation required to spread flow and prevent erosion

## Conveyance & Pretreatment
- Drainage layer and roof drains must safely convey overflow from large storms
- No pretreatment requirements

## Design Criteria

### Layer Specifications
Each layer (growing media, drainage layer, filter fabric, waterproofing) must meet ASTM specifications. Drainage layer may be combined with growing media layer.

### Structures Above Green Roof
Solar panels, HVAC equipment, and other structures may be installed above the green roof provided minimum clearance dimensions are maintained.

### Sizing Equation
```
Sv = (SA × d × η₁) / 12 + (SA × DL × η₂) / 12
```
- **Sv** = storage volume (ft³)
- **SA** = green roof area (ft²)
- **d** = media depth (in), minimum 3 in.
- **η₁** = media max. water retention (default: 0.10 if unverified)
- **DL** = drainage layer depth (in); = 0 if combined with media layer
- **η₂** = drainage layer max. water retention (default: 0.00 if unverified)

## Landscaping Criteria
Plants must resist and withstand: drought, fire, wind, snow loading, and heat stress.

- Temporary irrigation recommended during establishment
- **Permanent irrigation is prohibited** for extensive green roofs
- Intensive green roofs with permanent irrigation receive a reduced (50%) retention value

## Retention Value Calculations

| Condition | Retention Value |
|---|---|
| Unirrigated (extensive or intensive) | 100% of Sv (storage in media + drainage layer) |
| Irrigated (intensive only) | 50% of Sv |
