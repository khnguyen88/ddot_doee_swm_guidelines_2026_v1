Appendix E Stormwater Retention Credit Forms
(Certification, Trading, and Retirement)
Use the following forms to certify, transfer, and retire Stormwater Retention Credits (SRCs):
 Application for Certification of Stormwater Retention Credits
 Application for Transfer of Stormwater Retention Credit Ownership
 Application to Retire Stormwater Retention Credits
Applicants must submit forms through the Stormwater Database at https://doee.dc.gov/swdb.
Paper forms are also available in this appendix for reference (see Figures E.1 through E.3).
