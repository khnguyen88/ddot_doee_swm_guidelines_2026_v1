Appendix X Resources
The following documents provide more detailed information on many aspects of best
management practice (BMP) design than is found in this guidebook. These resources may be
useful for those looking to develop greater understanding of individual BMPs or stormwater
design in general. Recommendations in these resources may be used to inform BMP designs;
however, where conflicts occur between these resources and this guidebook, the requirements of
this guidebook prevail.
