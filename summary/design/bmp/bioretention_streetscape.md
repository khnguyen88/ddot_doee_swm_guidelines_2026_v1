# Bioretention — Streetscape / Structured (Curb Extension & Planter)

**Sources:**
- `docs/2014-0421-DDOT Green Infrastructure Standards.pdf` — Sections 33.14.5.1–33.14.5.7
- `docs/DOEE Stormwater Management Guidebook January 2020.pdf` — Section 3.6 (B-2, B-3, B-4)

---

## Overview

Structured streetscape bioretention covers three DDOT facility subtypes, all of which are subsets of DOEE's Streetscape Bioretention:

| DDOT Type | DOEE Variant | Typical Location | Sides |
|-----------|-------------|------------------|-------|
| Curb Extension Bioretention | B-2 Streetscape | Parking lane cut-in; may include tree space | Sloped or vertical |
| Streetscape Bioretention Planter | B-2 / B-4 | Between curb and sidewalk; offline | Usually vertical |
| Stormwater Planter | B-4 | Adjacent to building downspout; fully contained | Vertical with liner |
| Engineered Tree Pit | B-3 | Sidewalk tree zone | Covered/grated top |

## Soil Profile (Streetscape / Planter)

| Layer | Specification |
|-------|--------------|
| Mulch (top) | 3 in triple-shredded hardwood mulch |
| Filter media | 18–36 in bioretention soil (80–90% sand, 10–20% fines, 3–5% organic) |
| Choker layer | 2–4 in No. 8 or No. 89 washed stone over underdrain stone |
| Underdrain stone | ASTM D448 No. 57 or smaller; 2 in above and below pipe; max combined depth 12 in |
| Optional infiltration sump | No. 57 stone below underdrain invert; must drain within 72 hr |

**Filter media hydraulic conductivity:** 2–6 in/hr (ASTM D2434).  
**Compaction:** 84% max dry density (DDOT bioretention soil spec).

## Liner Requirements

| Location | Liner Required |
|----------|---------------|
| Within 10 ft of any structure/building | Yes — on structure side |
| Within 5 ft of roadway face of curb | Yes — on road side |
| Hot spots (DOEE-defined) | Yes — all sides; underdrain required |
| Stormwater planter (filter planter type) | Yes — watertight bottom, no infiltration |
| Infiltration planter | No bottom liner; place min 10 ft from building |

## Safety and Edge Conditions

| Condition | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Vertical drop adjacent to sidewalk, high-volume pedestrian | Railing required: top 18 in above sidewalk; ADA spacing for visually impaired |
| Vertical drop adjacent to sidewalk, low-volume pedestrian | Min 4-in high × 6-in wide curb |
| Vertical drop — parking step-out side | Min 4-in curb or railing |
| Sloped side — buffer at sidewalk | Flat (≤ 8%) buffer, min 6-in wide, different material |
| Sloped side — buffer at sidewalk, depth > 3 ft | Flat (≤ 8%) buffer, min 24-in wide |
| Adjacent to shared-use path (bike lane) | Flat (≤ 6:1) 24-in buffer; or max 3:1 slope with max 5-ft depth or 12-in drop; else 42-in guard |
| Depth > 5 ft, sloped sides | 36-in fence around entire facility |
| Vertical drop > 30 in | 42-in railing per IBC 1013 |
| Parking step-out zone width | 12–36 in from face of curb (optimal 24 in); min 18 in in high-volume areas |
| Pedestrian crossings (continuous facility) | 1 paved 6-ft crossing per tree spacing (max 35 ft) in high-traffic areas; every other tree elsewhere (max 70 ft) |

## Underdrain and Drainage

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Pipe type | 4- or 6-in perforated Sched. 40 PVC (corrugated HDPE ok for small BMPs) |
| Perforations | 3–4 rows of 3/8-in at 6-in o.c. |
| Underdrain spacing (wide cells) | Max 20 ft between pipes or edge of BMP |
| Clean-outs | Min 1 per basin/curb extension; within 10 ft of underdrain at catch basin connection |
| Connection | Catch basin, manhole, or direct storm sewer; backflow preventer if connected to CSS |
| Observation well | Min 1 per BMP; 4–6-in PVC; extends to top of ponding |

| BMP Subtype | Pretreatment Required |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Curb extension | Concrete or stone entry forebay per DDOT standard drawings |
| Streetscape planter | Stone forebay/splash block at curb cut; trash rack or leaf screen across curb cut |
| Online facility | Overflow structure sized for 15-yr storm; min 3-in freeboard |

---

## Stormwater Planter — Additional Requirements (B-4)

- Placed at grade or above ground
- Min filter media depth: 18 in
- **Infiltration planter:** setback min 10 ft from building; no bottom liner
- **Filter planter:** watertight shell or PVC liner on bottom; can be located adjacent to building; underdrain required to storm sewer
- Overflow pipe required to avoid spill-over
- Constructed of stone, concrete, brick, wood (avoid treated wood with trace metals)
