# Open Channel Systems (Linear) — Design Guidance Summary

**Sources:**
- `docs/DOEE Stormwater Management Guidebook January 2020.pdf` — Section 3.9
- `docs/2014-0421-DDOT Green Infrastructure Standards.pdf` — Sections 33.14.5 (bioswale), 47.7–47.8

---

## BMP Types

| Code | Type | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| O-1 | Grass Channel | Earthen, grass-lined; conveyance + modest filtering; relies on native soil permeability |
| O-2 | Dry Swale / Bioswale | Bioretention cell in linear form; filter media + underdrain; temporary ponding, no standing water |
| O-3 | Wet Swale | Permanent shallow wet pool; treats SWRv by extended detention; best on low-permeability soils |

**Note:** Open channel systems shall not be designed to provide stormwater detention except under extremely unusual conditions. Channels must generally be combined with a downstream retention/filtration BMP.

## Feasibility Criteria (§3.9.1)

| Parameter | Limit | Applicable Types | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-----------------|-------|
| Max CDA | 2.5 acres (preferred) | O-1, O-2, O-3 | Check dams required along entire length for CDA > 2.5 ac |
| Hydraulic head — wet swale/grass channel | Measured from inflow to outflow elevation | O-1, O-3 | Low head situations acceptable |
| Hydraulic head — dry swale | 3–5 ft required (filter media resistance) | O-2 | Check dam or grade change required to create head |
| Min depth to seasonal high water table | 2 ft below BMP bottom | O-1, O-2 | Not applicable to O-3 (wet pool by design) |
| Setback to structures / property lines | 10 ft min | O-2 | Liner required on adjacent side if < 10 ft |
| Impermeable liner trigger | Sites with contaminated soil, fill applications, or setback < 10 ft | O-2 (required), O-3 (recommended) | PVC geomembrane or equivalent |
| Hotspot runoff | Not treated by O-2 (infiltrating) or O-3 (wet pool) without liner | O-2, O-3 | O-1 may convey but does not treat; liner required for O-2 on hotspot sites |
| Irrigated/baseflow inputs | Avoid — locate to prevent inputs from springs, irrigation, chlorinated wash water | All | — |

## Channel Geometry (§3.9.4)

| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Cross-section shape | Trapezoidal or parabolic | Parabolic preferred for aesthetics, maintenance, and hydraulics |
| Bottom width | 4–8 ft | If > 8 ft, add benches, check dams, level spreaders, or multi-level cross sections to prevent braiding |
| Side slopes — O-1, O-2 (grass) | No steeper than 3H:1V | Flatter preferred where space allows; required for mowing |
| Side slopes — O-3 (wet swale) | No steeper than 3H:1V (absolute minimum); 4H:1V or flatter preferred | Flatter enables wetland plant growth |
| Alignment | Adjacent to and same length as the CDA being treated | — |
| Maximum ponding depth — O-2 | 18 in max; 3 in min (averaged over surface) | Measured at check dam |
| Maximum ponding depth — O-3 | 18 in temporary; 6 in max normal pool depth (permanent, dry weather) | — |
| Flow depth — O-1 (SWRv design storm) | Max 4 in for sizing purposes | — |
| Freeboard | 6 in min above design water surface profile of channel | Applies to 15-year storm; check at check dam overflow too |

### Check Dam Design Rules

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Maximum check dam height | 12 in typical; 18 in allowed with additional structural design |
| Minimum segment length between check dams | 25–40 ft |
| Center weir sizing | Pass 15-year storm peak flow |
| Anchoring | Firmly anchored into side slopes (no outflanking) and into channel bottom (no hydrostatic push) |
| Downstream toe armoring | Required where needed to prevent scour |
| Weep holes — O-1 grass channel | Required at channel invert for dewatering; at least 3 per dam for slopes < 2% |
| Weep holes — O-2 dry swale | Not appropriate (ponding required) |
| Materials | Wood (pressure-treated or water-resistant species: cedar, hemlock, swamp oak, locust), concrete, gabions, riprap, compacted soil, elevated culverts |

## Pretreatment Requirements (§3.9.3)

| Pretreatment Type | Application | Volume Requirement |
|-------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Check dams (channel flow) | CDA < 1 ac | 15% of design volume in pretreatment zone |
| Tree check dams (channel flow) | Street trees as mounds 9–12 in above invert; gravel bypass one side | 15% of design volume |
| Grass filter strip (sheet flow) | 5H:1V (20%) slope or flatter; or 5 ft at max 5% cross-slope + 3H:1V side slopes | — |
| Gravel/stone diaphragm (sheet flow) | Along pavement edge; 2–4 in drop from hard surface into gravel | Stone sized for expected discharge |
| Gravel/stone flow spreader (concentrated) | At curb cuts, downspouts; 2–4 in drop into diaphragm spanning full opening width | Level stone weir at bottom/treatment elevation |
| Initial sediment forebay (channel flow) | At upper end of channel; 2:1 L:W ratio | ≥ 15% of total design volume; must dewater between storms if volume counted toward Sv |

## Soil Profile and Filter Media

### O-2 Dry Swale Filter Media (same recipe as bioretention — §3.6)

| Component | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Composition | 80–90% sand; 10–20% soil fines; max 10% clay; 3–5% organic matter |
| Minimum filter media depth | 18 in above choker stone layer; max 6 ft |
| Alternative (turf cover) | 100% sand for first 18 in; topsoil + compost per Appendix K for top 4 in |
| Filter media testing | P content: 5–15 mg/kg (Mehlich I) or 18–40 mg/kg (Mehlich III); CEC > 5 meq/100 g |
| Drawdown requirement | Entire design storm volume filtered within 72 hours |

### Underdrain (O-2 Dry Swale)

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Pipe | 4–6 in rigid Schedule 40 PVC; 3 or 4 rows of 3/8 in perforations at 6 in o.c. |
| Choker layer (above underdrain stone) | 2–4 in of No. 8 or No. 89 washed gravel |
| Underdrain stone layer | No. 57 stone (or No. 68/8/89) washed, free of fines (< 2% passing No. 200); min 2 in above and below underdrain pipe |
| Maximum total underdrain + choker depth | 12 in combined; shall not extend beyond surface dimensions of filter media |
| Geotextile | AASHTO M-288 Class 2; permeability ≥ 10× subgrade; apply to sides only — not bottom |
| Underdrain requirement | Required for HSG D soils; may omit where native infiltration rate meets minimum standard |
| Caps | Cap upstream ends of underdrain; do not cap downstream end |

### O-1 Grass Channel — Compost Soil Amendment (optional)

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| Application | Incorporated to depth per Appendix K; applied over full bottom width and length |
| Purpose | Increases retention capability |
| Stabilization | Perennial, salt-tolerant grass species; biodegradable erosion control mat on steep slopes |

## Hydraulic Sizing Equations (§3.9.6)

### O-1 Grass Channel

```
Equation 3.17 — Manning's Equation:
  V = (1.49/n) × D^(2/3) × S^(1/2)

  V = flow velocity (ft/s)
  n = roughness coefficient (0.2 at D ≤ 4 in; 0.03 at D ≥ 12 in)
  D = flow depth (ft); approximates hydraulic radius for shallow flow
  S = channel slope (ft/ft)

Equation 3.18 — Continuity (trapezoidal, 3H:1V sides):
  Q = V × (W + 3×D) × D

  Q = design storm peak flow rate (cfs)
  W = channel bottom width (ft)

Equation 3.19 — Minimum Bottom Width:
  W = [n × Q / (1.49 × D^(5/3) × S^(1/2))] − (3 × D)

Equation 3.20 — Velocity Check:
  V = Q / [(W + 3×D) × D]

Equation 3.21 — Minimum Length for 9-min HRT:
  L = 540 × V

  L = minimum swale length (ft)
  V = flow velocity (ft/s)
  540 = 9 minutes × 60 sec/min

Equation 3.22 — Grass Channel Storage Volume:
  Sv = DesignStorm (ft³)
  [volume of SWRv or design storm conveyed at ≤ 4 in depth]
```

### O-2 Dry Swale

```
Equation 3.23 — Dry Swale Storage Volume:
  Sv = SAbottom × [(dmedia × ηmedia) + (dgravel × ηgravel)] + (SAaverage × dponding)

  Sv       = total storage volume (ft³)
  SAbottom = bottom surface area of dry swale (ft²)
  dmedia   = depth of filter media including mulch (ft)
  ηmedia   = effective porosity of filter media (typically 0.25)
  dgravel  = depth of gravel layer + choker stone (ft)
  ηgravel  = effective porosity of gravel (typically 0.40)
  SAaverage = average surface area = (SAtop + SAbottom) / 2
  dponding = max ponding depth (ft); must not exceed 18 in (1.5 ft)

Note: Ponded surface area (at top of ponding) may not exceed 2× SAbottom.
```

### O-3 Wet Swale

```
Equation 3.24 — Wet Swale Storage Volume:
  Sv = Pond permanent pool volume + 24-hr extended detention volume

  Sv must not exceed design SWRv.
  Temporary ponding ≤ 12 in above permanent pool; 24-hr extended detention.
```

### Planting Rules

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|--------------|
| Target cover | 90% vegetated cover after 2nd growing season |
| O-1 grass channel | Seed only — do not sod (sod muck impedes infiltration; seeds form deeper roots) |
| O-3 wet swale | Grass + wetland species; reference §3.11 Stormwater Wetlands plant list |
| Salt exposure | Salt-tolerant species required if contributing surface receives winter road salt |
| Erosion control | Biodegradable erosion control fabric during establishment |
| Mowing maintenance | Maintain grass at 4–6 in height during growing season |
