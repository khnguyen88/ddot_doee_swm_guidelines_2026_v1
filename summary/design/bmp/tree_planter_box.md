# Engineered Tree Planter Box / Tree Pit (B-3)

**Sources:**
- `docs/2014-0421-DDOT Green Infrastructure Standards.pdf` — Sections 33.14.5.1, 47.7, 47.7.4–47.7.7
- `docs/DOEE Stormwater Management Guidebook January 2020.pdf` — Section 3.6 (B-3), Section 3.14

---

## Overview

Engineered tree boxes (DOEE B-3) are bioretention installations in the sidewalk tree zone near the street. The enlarged soil volume captures and treats stormwater while also supporting tree growth. Surface coverage may be mulch, grates, permeable pavers, or conventional pavement. Connected boxes in a row increase treatment effectiveness.

---

## Dimensions and Spacing

| Parameter | New/Reconstructed Streetscape | Confined/Existing Sidewalk |
|-----------|-------------------------------|---------------------------|
| Minimum open tree pit (length × width) | 6 ft × 4 ft | 9 ft × 6 ft |
| Minimum continuous open area width | 4 ft | 6 ft |
| Soil profile depth (below pavement) | Min 30 in SBSS or Bioretention Soil (see covered soil) | Same |

---

## Minimum Soil Volumes (DDOT 47.7.1)

| Tree Size | Min Soil Volume | Max Radius |
|-----------|----------------|------------|
| Large tree | 1,500 cu ft | 27 ft |
| Medium tree | 1,000 cu ft | 22 ft |
| Small tree | 600 cu ft | 16 ft |

- Adjacent trees may share up to 25% of required volume where their radius zones overlap.
- Calculation: (Open area × depth) + (Covered area × depth).

---

## Soil Profile (DDOT 47.7.5)

| Configuration | Profile |
|---------------|---------|
| Open plant bed over SBSS | 12 in Plant Bed Soil + min 24 in Sand-Based Structural Soil (SBSS) |
| All Plant Bed Soil | Min 36 in Plant Bed Soil |
| Turf strip over SBSS | 12 in Lawn Soil + min 24 in SBSS |
| Covered soil volume (under pavement) | 30 in SBSS + 6 in open-graded aeration aggregate (No. 8 or No. 57) at top |
| Suspended pavement (structural slab) | Min 30 in Bioretention Soil or per manufacturer |
| Structural cells (Silva Cell, etc.) | Min 30 in Bioretention Soil inside cells; per manufacturer |

**Sub-soil:** Loosen by tilling/ripping before placing structural soil layers.

---

## Bioretention Credit When Used for Stormwater (DOEE B-3 / DDOT 47.7.6)

When engineered tree pits include ponding volume, they qualify as DOEE "Engineered Tree Box" bioretention:

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Filter media depth — standard | Min 18 in (not including mulch) |
| Filter media depth — enhanced | Min 24 in |
| Min ponding depth (avg. over BMP surface) | 3 in; if covered pavement doubles surface area, must be 6 in |
| Filter media composition | Same as bioretention (80–90% sand, 10–20% fines, 3–5% organic) |
| Compaction for SBSS used as bioretention media | 84% max dry density; if > 84%, porosity reduced to 0.10 |
| Phosphorus content — SBSS as filter media | Must meet same P limits as standard bioretention filter media |
| Drawdown time | Max 72 hr |

## Covered Soil Volume — Stormwater Conveyance (DDOT 47.7.7)

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Stormwater input to covered soil | 0.5× to 4.0× the open surface area of soil |
| Preferred method | Permeable pavement over SBSS/structural cell |
| Alternative (no permeable pavement) | Linear drains at 8-ft intervals, or catch basins with perforated distribution pipes |
| Impervious cover ≤ 6 ft wide | Linear drains or catch basins optional |
| Aeration layer over all covered soils | Min 6 in uniformly-graded crushed stone (No. 8 or No. 57) |

---

## Subsurface Drainage Requirements (DDOT 47.7.7)

| Native Soil Infiltration Rate | NRCS Soil Group | Underdrain Requirement |
|-------------------------------|-----------------|------------------------|
| > 0.5 in/hr | A or B | None required |
| 0.14–0.5 in/hr | C | 12 in sand layer beneath tree soil volume |
| < 0.14 in/hr | D | 12 in sand + 4-in perforated underdrain with geotextile surround; connect to storm drain |

## Safety, Barriers, and Access

| Condition | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Open tree pit — pedestrian protection | Ornamental fencing per DDOT requirements |
| Tree pit with vertical drop adjacent to sidewalk (high-traffic) | 18-in railing; ADA-compliant spacing |
| Tree pit with vertical drop adjacent to sidewalk (low-traffic) | Min 4-in curb |
| Grate over filter media | Removable grate allows tree growth-through; may use wrought iron fence |
| Pedestrian crossings | 1 paved 6-ft crossing per tree spacing (max 35 ft) in high-traffic areas |
| Parking egress strip | 12–36 in from face of curb; only in metered parking zones with ≥ 12 ft total public space width |

---

## Liner Requirements

- Within **10 ft of a building/structure:** liner on structure side
- Within **5 ft of roadway face of curb:** liner on road side
- **Hot spots:** liner all sides + underdrain required
- Stormwater planter (B-4 filter variant): watertight base, no infiltration to ground
