# Tree Planting and Tree Space — Design Guidance Summary

**Sources:**
- `docs/2014-0421-DDOT Green Infrastructure Standards.pdf` — Sections 47.7–47.8
- `docs/DOEE Stormwater Management Guidebook January 2020.pdf` — Section 3.14 (Tree Planting and Preservation)

---

## Overview

Street trees in the DC public right-of-way must be selected from DDOT-approved plant lists and placed in adequate soil volume. Two configurations exist:

- **Open tree space:** Soil exposed at surface (mulched or turf); most cost-effective for achieving required volume.
- **Covered soil volume:** Soil beneath adjacent pavement supported by suspended pavements, structural cells, or structural soils (SBSS/CU Soil).

Both approaches can be designed to meet DOEE stormwater retention requirements when ponding volume is incorporated (engineered tree box, B-3).

---

## Minimum Soil Volumes (DDOT 47.7.1)

| Tree Size | Minimum Soil Volume | Maximum Radius |
|-----------|---------------------|---------------|
| Large | 1,500 cu ft | 27 ft |
| Medium | 1,000 cu ft | 22 ft |
| Small | 600 cu ft | 16 ft |

- Adjacent trees sharing a continuous soil zone: up to 25% of each tree's required volume may overlap.
- Formula: `(Open area × depth) + (Covered area × depth)`; all soil types counted at full volume.

## Minimum Tree Pit Dimensions

| Context | Minimum Open Pit Size |
|---------|----------------------|
| New or reconstructed streetscape (soil volume under pavement) | 4 ft wide × 6 ft long |
| Existing confined sidewalk (no soil under pavement) | 6 ft wide × 9 ft long |
| Continuous open area adjacent to sidewalk | No minimum length specified; width per above |

## Soil Profile Specifications (DDOT 47.7.5)

| Use Case | Soil Profile |
|----------|-------------|
| Open plant bed (new) | 12 in Plant Bed Soil + min 24 in SBSS; or 36 in Plant Bed Soil (all open) |
| Turf infiltration strip | 12 in Lawn Soil + min 24 in SBSS; or 36 in Lawn Soil |
| Covered soil — open tree area | 12 in Plant Bed Soil + 24 in SBSS (in open zone) |
| Covered soil — below impervious pavement | 30 in SBSS + 6 in open-graded aeration aggregate (No. 8 or No. 57) at top |
| Suspended pavement system | Min 30 in Bioretention Soil beneath slab; per manufacturer for commercial systems |
| Structural cell system (e.g., Silva Cell) | Min 30 in Bioretention Soil inside cells; per manufacturer certification |
| CU Soil | Proprietary; from licensed facility only; NOT for stormwater infiltration |

## Acceptable Covered Soil Systems (DDOT 47.7.4)

| System | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| Suspended pavement | Structural slab on supports; structural calcs required; 30 in soil beneath |
| Structural cells | Commercially available; manufacturer details and cert. required |
| Sand-Based Structural Soil (SBSS) | Min 6 in open-graded crushed stone over min 30 in SBSS; aeration and water supply required |
| CU Soil | Patented; licensed facility only; no use with stormwater infiltration |
| Stalite Structural Soil | Proprietary lightweight aggregate product |

## Stormwater Conveyance to Covered Soil (DDOT 47.7.7)

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Input area : covered soil area ratio | 0.5× to 4.0× open surface area |
| Preferred drainage method | Permeable pavement directly over structural soil |
| Alternative (no permeable pavement) | Linear drains at 8-ft spacing; or catch basins with perforated distribution pipes |
| Impervious cover ≤ 6 ft wide | Linear drains or catch basins optional |
| Aeration layer (all covered soils) | Min 6 in No. 8 or No. 57 stone at top |

| Native Soil Infiltration Rate | NRCS Hydrologic Soil Group | Underdrain |
|-------------------------------|---------------------------|------------|
| > 0.5 in/hr | A or B | None |
| 0.14–0.5 in/hr | C | 12 in sand layer below tree soil volume |
| < 0.14 in/hr | D | 12 in sand + 4-in perforated underdrain with geotextile surround; connect to storm drain |

## Access, Safety, and Pedestrian Crossings (DDOT 47.7.8)

| Condition | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Pedestrian crossings (continuous open strip, high-traffic) | Paved 6-ft crossing between every tree |
| Pedestrian crossings (continuous open strip, low-traffic) | Alternating every other tree; matching surface material |
| Parking egress strip | 12–36 in from face of curb; only in metered zones with ≥ 12 ft total public space width |
| Ornamental fencing | May be required to protect planting soil from foot traffic; combine with bike racks per DDOT PM |
| Plant height (between curb and sidewalk, not trees) | < 18 in above sidewalk level |

## Planting Guidance (DDOT 47.8)

Selection criteria for plants in vegetated stormwater systems:
- **Light:** full sun, partial shade, full shade
- **Water:** match inundation tolerance to frequency of ponding/drainage
- **Salt tolerance:** high tolerance preferred near winter-treated roads
- **Pollution tolerance:** more maintenance required in high-pollution corridors
- **Size:** appropriate mature size for site; maintain sight lines and pedestrian/vehicle safety

Plants (excluding trees) between curb and sidewalk must be < 18 in above sidewalk at maturity.

Refer to DDOT Green Infrastructure Standards Section 04 (Plant List) for the current approved species list organized by: light, water, salt, pollution, maintenance category (low/medium/high), and facility type (bioretention, permeable pavement, tree space).

## Utility Clearances (DC Water GIUPG and DDOT 33.14.6)

**Sources:**
- *DC Water Green Infrastructure Utility Protection Guidelines*, July 2013, Version 1.0 — `DC_Water_Green_Infrastructure_Utility_Protection_Guidelines_2013.pdf`
  - Dwg 1 of 8 "Water and Sewer Clearances for Trees" (PDF p. 2)
- *DDOT Green Infrastructure Standards* (2014) — `2014-0421-DDOT Green Infrastructure Standards.pdf`, §33.14.6 (pp. D-21–D-23)

**DC Water general rule (Dwg 1 of 8, PDF p. 2):** Structural/proprietary devices — including precast concrete tree boxes, structural soil framing systems, and cast-in-place walls — shall not be placed within **5 ft horizontally** of the outer edge of any DC Water utility. Exception: utility > 15 ft below finished grade, or otherwise approved by DC Water.

| Element | Utility | Horizontal Clearance | Vertical | Notes | Source |
|---------|---------|---------------------|---------|-------|--------|

| Tree trunk (breast height) | Service lateral | 3 ft min | — | | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 1 of 8 (PDF p. 2) |
| Precast concrete tree box | Water or sewer main | 5 ft min (face of box to outer edge of utility) | — | Exception if utility > 15 ft deep or DC Water approval | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 1 of 8 (PDF p. 2) |
