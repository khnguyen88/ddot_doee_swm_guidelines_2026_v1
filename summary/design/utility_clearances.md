# Utility Clearance Requirements — DC Water, DDOT, and Green Infrastructure

**Sources:**
- `docs/DC_Water_Green_Infrastructure_Utility_Protection_Guidelines_2013.pdf` — July 2013, Version 1.0 (DC Water GIUPG)
- `docs/2014-0421-DDOT Green Infrastructure Standards.pdf` — Section 33.14.6 (DDOT GI Standards)
- `summary/DC_Water_GI_Utility_Protection_Guidelines_2013.md`
- `summary/design/bmp/` — Individual BMP design files

**Applies to:** Public right-of-way projects in Washington, DC involving green infrastructure BMPs adjacent to or crossing underground utilities.

---

## Regulatory Basis

| Authority | Citation | Scope |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| DC Water GIUPG | DCMR 12 §102.2 | Water mains, sewer mains, service laterals, meters, shutoffs, valve boxes, cleanouts, hydrants |
| DDOT GI Standards | Section 33.14.6 | All ROW utilities: gas, communications, power, street lights, hydrants adjacent to GI BMPs |

**General rule (DC Water):** Proprietary structural devices — structural soil framing systems, precast concrete boxes, cast-in-place walls — shall not be placed within **5 ft horizontally** of the outer edge of any DC Water utility. Waivers require DC Water review and approval.

## 1. Storm Sewer (Sanitary and Storm Drain) Clearances

### Bioretention and Bioswales

| Condition | Requirement | Source |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Bottom of facility to sewer **main** (vertical) | 12 in min | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 2 |
| Bottom of facility to sewer **lateral** (vertical) | 12 in min | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 2 |
| Vertical clearance < 5 ft to sewer main or lateral | Impermeable membrane + underdrain required for full perimeter | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 2 |
| Sewer main or lateral within **3 ft horizontally** of facility | Impermeable membrane + underdrain required for full perimeter | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 2 |
| Service lateral **crossing within** the facility | Impermeable membrane on 3 ft each side of pipe for full width | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 2 |
| If membrane/underdrain NOT used | Sewers and laterals must be lined from manhole to manhole | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 2 |
| Disturbing pipe bedding | Not permitted | DC Water GIUPG (all drawings) |

### Permeable Pavement

| Condition | Requirement | Source |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Bottom of reservoir to sewer **main** (vertical) | 12 in min | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 5 |
| Bottom of reservoir to sewer **lateral** (vertical) | 12 in min | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 5 |
| Vertical clearance < 5 ft to sewer | Impermeable membrane + underdrain required | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 5 |
| Membrane extent (when required) | Extend **3 ft beyond** utility on each side | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 5 |
| If membrane/underdrain NOT used | Sewers and laterals must be lined from manhole to manhole | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 5 |

### DDOT Supplement (§33.14.6)

| Utility | Horizontal Clearance | Vertical Clearance | Liner Trigger |
|---------|---------------------|--------------------|---------------|
| Water/sewer main — any BMP | 3 ft (liner zone) | 12 in min | Liner + underdrain if < 5 ft vertical OR < 3 ft horizontal |

## 5. Utility Surface Structures (Manholes, Valve Boxes, Cleanouts)

### In Bioretention and Bioswales

| Parameter | Requirement | Source |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Concrete collar — top elevation | Match top of roadside curb; must be **above design ponding depth** | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 4 |
| Horizontal clearance around structure | 8 in min | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 4 |
| Top of utility structure below BMP finished grade | 12 in min below grade | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 4 |
| Manholes inside bioretention area | **Not allowed** | DDOT §33.14.6 |

### In Permeable Pavement

| Parameter | Requirement | Source |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Concrete collar — top elevation | Match pavement grade; above ponding depth | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 6 |
| Concrete pad/collar width around structure | 8 in min | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 6 |
| Top of structure below pavement grade | 12 in min | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 6 |
| Manholes in permeable pavement | **Allowed** — concrete collar required; top above ponding depth | DDOT §33.14.6 |

## 10. Underdrain Connection Rules (Catch Basins vs. Manholes)

| Condition | Requirement | Source |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Preferred underdrain outlet | Existing manhole or sewer line | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 8 |
| Catch basin connection allowed if: | GI facility > 20 ft from nearest manhole/sewer line **OR** total GI surface area < 250 sq ft | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 8 |
| Private property underdrains to catch basin | **Prohibited** | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 8 |
| Max distance from catch basin to underdrain outlet | 5 ft | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 8 |
| Max lateral offset of underdrain from catch basin wall | 8 in | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 8 |
| Solid underdrain invert above outlet pipe crown | 6 in min | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 8 |
| Pipe end at catch basin wall | Flush to wall; removable hood with screen required | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 8 |
| Min catch basin bottom to outlet pipe | 6 in | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 8 |
| Clay dams / waterstops along underdrain | Max 100 ft spacing (min one) | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 8 |
| Clean-out spacing | Max 100 ft; storm drain structures within 100 ft count as clean-outs | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 8 |
| Clean-out near catch basin | Min one located ≤ 5 ft from catch basin | DC Water GIUPG Dwg 8 |

## Key Notes

1. All GI measures shall meet applicable DDOT, DOEE, and DC Water standards simultaneously.
2. No GI practice shall disturb pipe bedding as defined in DC Water's Design Manual.
3. Waivers from DC Water GIUPG are subject to DC Water review and approval only.
4. DC Water is **not responsible** for clogging or cleaning of underdrains.
5. Water service pipes > 2 in diameter are treated as water mains (12 in vertical clearance applies).
6. If impermeable membrane and underdrain are NOT used near sewer, sewers and laterals must be lined from manhole to manhole — a significantly more expensive alternative.
7. DDOT IPMA approval is required for all permeable pavement installations in PROW regardless of utility conflicts.
