# Major Substantial Improvement (MSI) — Definition and Stormwater Requirements

**Sources:**
- `docs/21 DCMR Chapter 5_11_14_2025.pdf` — § 599 Definitions (primary regulatory authority)
- `docs/DOEE Stormwater Management Guidebook January 2020.pdf` — Chapter 1 (p. 1), Table 2-2 (p. 10), Sections 2.6–2.7 (pp. 20–21)
- `docs/doee presentation/01_Stormwater Management Requirements.pptx`

## Overview

A Major Substantial Improvement (MSI) is a renovation or improvement to an existing building or structure where the construction cost is large relative to the building's assessed value AND the combined project footprint meets a size threshold. MSI is one of two project types that triggers DC stormwater management (SWM) requirements under 21 DCMR Chapter 5; the other is Major Land-Disturbing Activity (MLD). MSI applies to existing structures — it is triggered by cost relative to building value, not by soil disturbance alone.

---

## Step 1 — "Substantial Improvement" (Cost Threshold)

> **"Substantial improvement"** — A repair, alteration, addition, or improvement of a building or structure, the cost of which **equals or exceeds fifty percent (50%) of the market value of the structure** before the improvement or repair is started.
>
> *21 DCMR § 599.1*

**Market value** is defined as:

> The assessed value of the structure for the most recent year, as recorded in the real property assessment database maintained by the **District of Columbia's Office of Tax and Revenue (OTR)**.
>
> *21 DCMR § 599.1*

**Formula:** Project construction cost ≥ 50% × OTR-assessed structural value

---

## Step 2 — "Major" (Size Threshold — What Triggers SWM Regulation)

> **"Major substantial improvement activity"** — Substantial improvement activity and associated land-disturbing activity, including such activities that are part of a common plan of development, for which the **combined footprint of improved building and land-disturbing activity is 5,000 ft² or greater**, and:
>
> (a) Some area of the pre-project land cover is natural; **or**
>
> (b) 2,500 ft² or greater of the post-project land cover is impervious.
>
> *A major substantial improvement activity may include a substantial improvement activity that is not associated with land disturbance.*
>
> *21 DCMR § 599.1*

---

## Combined Trigger Checklist

Both conditions must be satisfied simultaneously:

| Condition | Threshold | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost test** | Construction cost ≥ 50% of OTR-assessed structure market value | 21 DCMR § 599.1 ("Substantial improvement") |
| **Size test — footprint** | Combined building footprint + land disturbance ≥ 5,000 ft² | 21 DCMR § 599.1 ("Major substantial improvement activity") |
| **Size test — land cover** (either/or) | Pre-project natural cover exists **OR** ≥ 2,500 ft² post-project impervious | 21 DCMR § 599.1 |

## Stormwater Requirements Once Triggered

| Requirement | MSI Obligation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **SWRv rainfall depth (non-AWDZ)** | **0.8 inches** (80th percentile storm) | Guidebook Table 2-2, p. 10 |
| **SWRv rainfall depth (AWDZ)** | **1.0 inches** (85th percentile storm) | Guidebook Table 2-2, p. 10 |
| **WQTv (AWDZ publicly financed only)** | Additional volume to 1.7" storm | Guidebook Section 2.4, p. 14 |
| **2-year storm detention** | **Exempt** | Guidebook Section 2.6, p. 20 |
| **15-year storm detention** | **Exempt** | Guidebook Section 2.7, p. 21 |
| **On-site retention minimum (MS4)** | 50% of SWRv must be on-site | Guidebook Section 2.3, p. 11 |
| **On-site retention minimum (CSS)** | No mandatory on-site percentage | Guidebook Section 2.3, p. 11 |

### SWRv Formula

```
SWRv (gal) = P/12 × (0.95×I + 0.25×C + 0.0×N) × 7.48
```

Where P = 0.8" (non-AWDZ) or 1.0" (AWDZ) for MSI projects.

---

## Comparison: MSI vs. Major Land-Disturbing Activity (MLD)

| | **MSI** | **MLD** |
|---|---|---|
| **Trigger basis** | Cost ≥ 50% of market value of existing structure | Soil disturbance ≥ 5,000 ft² |
| **SWRv rainfall (non-AWDZ)** | **0.8 inches** | **1.2 inches** |
| **SWRv rainfall (AWDZ)** | **1.0 inches** | **1.2 inches** |
| **2-yr/15-yr detention** | **Exempt** | Required |
| **Applicable to new construction?** | No — existing structures only | Yes |

> "Major land-disturbing activities must retain the volume from a 1.2-inch storm event, and major substantial improvement activities must retain the volume from a 0.8-inch storm event."
>
> *DOEE SWM Guidebook, Chapter 1, p. 1*

---

## Key Points / Notes

- **A project can trigger both MSI and MLD simultaneously** on different portions of the same site (e.g., a renovation that also disturbs ≥ 5,000 ft² of soil). Each area must be evaluated separately with its applicable P value.
- **The 50% cost test uses structural value only**, not land value — use the OTR-assessed structure value, not total property assessment.
- **MSI may include work not associated with land disturbance** — e.g., a building addition entirely on existing impervious surface with no soil disturbance can still qualify if the footprint threshold is met.
- **"Common plan of development"** — multiple phased activities count together toward the 5,000 ft² footprint threshold if they share a common development plan. *(21 DCMR § 599.1)*
- The lower SWRv rainfall depth for MSI (0.8" vs. 1.2" for MLD) reflects recognition that retrofit of existing developed sites faces more constraints than new construction.
