# DC Water Green Infrastructure Utility Protection Guidelines (2013)

**Source:** `docs/DC_Water_Green_Infrastructure_Utility_Protection_Guidelines_2013.pdf`  
**Date:** July 2013, Version 1.0  
**Authority:** DCMR 12, Section 102.2 — Public Works Standards  
**Applies to:** Street tree planting, tree box filters, bioretention, bioswales, permeable pavements and pavers, alleys with bioretention, underdrains adjacent to catch basins

---

## Overview

Provides standard details for designing green infrastructure adjacent to or crossing DC Water utilities (water mains, sewers, water/sewer service laterals, meters, shutoffs, valve boxes, cleanouts, hydrants).

**General rule:** Proprietary structural devices (structural soil framing systems, precast concrete boxes, cast-in-place walls) shall not be placed within **5 ft horizontally** of the outer edge of DC Water utilities.

Waivers require DC Water review and approval.

## Drawing 2 of 8 — Sewer Clearances for Bioretention and Bioswales

| Condition | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Bottom of facility to sewer **main** (vertical) | 12 in min |
| Bottom of facility to sewer **lateral** (vertical) | 12 in min |
| Vertical clearance < 5 ft to sewer main or lateral | Impermeable membrane + underdrain required for **full perimeter** of facility |
| Sewer main or lateral within **3 ft horizontally** of facility AND depth requirements not met | Impermeable membrane + underdrain required for full perimeter |
| Service lateral **crossing within** the facility | Impermeable membrane on 3 ft each side of pipe for full width of facility |

**Notes:**
1. Bioretention/bioswales shall not disturb pipe bedding per DC Water Design Manual.
2. If impermeable membrane and underdrain are NOT used, sewers and laterals must be **lined from manhole to manhole**.

## Drawing 3 of 8 — Water Clearances for Bioretention and Bioswales

| Condition | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Bottom of facility to water **main** (vertical) | 12 in min |
| Bottom of facility to water **service lateral** | No minimum vertical requirement |

**Notes:**
1. Bioretention/bioswales shall not disturb pipe bedding per DC Water Design Manual.
2. Water service pipes **> 2 in diameter** shall be considered water mains (12 in min clearance applies).
3. Water service laterals shall **not be exposed** and must have **18 in min soil cover**.

## Drawing 8 of 8 — Underdrains Adjacent to Catch Basins

### Connection Eligibility
| Condition | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Preferred underdrain connection point | Existing manhole or sewer line |
| Catch basin connection allowed only if: | GI facility is **> 20 ft** from existing manhole/sewer line, **OR** total surface area of all GI measures is **< 250 sq ft** |
| Private property underdrains to catch basin | **Prohibited** |

### Geometry at Catch Basin Connection
| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Max distance from catch basin to underdrain outlet | 5 ft |
| Max offset of underdrain from catch basin wall | 8 in |
| Solid underdrain invert above outlet pipe crown | 6 in min |
| Pipe end at catch basin wall | Flush to wall |
| Hood/screen | Removable hood with screen at pipe end |
| Min catch basin bottom to outlet pipe | 6 in |

### Underdrain System Requirements
| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Clay dams / waterstops along underdrain | 100 ft max spacing (min one); prevent migration of water along pipe |
| Clean-outs | 100 ft max spacing; min one located ≤ 5 ft from catch basin |
| Underdrain design purpose | Low-flow, subsurface drain for the GI measure only |
| Filtration | System must filter incoming water to prevent silt/turbidity migration to sewer |

### Cross-Section Dimensions (Typical Bioretention at Catch Basin)
| Layer | Depth |
|-------|-------|
| Ponding depth | 6–12 in |
| Soil media | ~18 in |
| Gravel layer | ~12 in |

## Key Notes Applicable to All Details

1. All GI measures shall meet applicable DDOT, DOEE, and DC Water standards.
2. No GI practice shall disturb pipe bedding as defined in DC Water's Design Manual.
3. Waivers from these guidelines are subject to review and approval solely at DC Water's discretion.
4. DC Water is **not responsible** for clogging or cleaning of underdrains.
5. Structural/proprietary devices (precast boxes, soil framing systems, cast-in-place walls) must be **≥ 5 ft horizontally** from any DC Water utility.
